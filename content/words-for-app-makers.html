Words for app makers

Labels for settings that IRL apps share. Each one is a recommendation for one context, not the only right word.

Part of the open data in irl-commons. The plain explanations are in the glossary on How to IRL.

How to read it

Use it for
The screen or setting the label fits.
Also used
Means the same thing.
Narrower
One part or one kind of it.
Technical
The engineering name.
Ambiguous here
In this context it can also mean something else.
Draft
Open for comment. A label is reviewed once the settings of at least two makers and two stewards back it.

Evidence is a public page or the app's own settings text, with the day it was read. Your product uses another label? Propose a different label.
