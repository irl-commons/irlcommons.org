Apps, backpacks and services for IRL streaming mostly don't work together.

  • Moving a stream from one to another, or checking that viewers still get it, often means connecting things by hand.
  • Many streamers rely on open tools that a handful of people maintain, often in their free time.
  • A lot of the chain is closed source, so each maker solves the same problems alone.

IRL Commons

Open initiative for shared formats and open data around IRL and hybrid streaming.

  • The initiativeWhy we need better standards and interoperability, and three ideas.
  • Open standardsOpen drafts for status, sessions and data. Comments welcome.
  • Open datairl-commons: words, services, guides and existing formats that anyone can reuse with credit.
  • ContributeAdd an app, a word or a guide, or share what breaks in your streams.
  • For streamers: How to IRLGuides, a glossary and an open list of apps and services, on howtoirl.org.
