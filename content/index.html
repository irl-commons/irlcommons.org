IRL Commons

Shared formats and open data for IRL streaming, so the apps, backpacks and services streamers already use can work together. Anyone can use them, and anyone can help improve them.

  • The initiativeWhat's still hard in IRL streaming, the ideas that could fix it, and where each part stands.
  • StandardsFormats, protocols and codecs that more than one app understands, with tables to compare them.
  • Open standardsFour open drafts for status, sessions and data. Comments are welcome.
  • Open datairl-commons: words, services, guides and standards that anyone can reuse with credit.
  • ContributeAdd an app, a word or a guide, or share what breaks in your streams.
  • For streamers: How to IRLPlain guides, a glossary and the link list, on howtoirl.org.

One project that builds on these is OpenStreamBox, the reference backpack build, with its working forks on GitHub.
